What Is Surfing?

Surfing is the sport of riding a wave, typically on a board, as it breaks toward the shore. It blends athleticism, balance, and an intimate reading of ocean conditions — wind, swell, and tide all shape what a surfer can do on any given day. Beyond the sport itself, surfing carries a rich culture built around respect for the ocean and the community that shares it.
