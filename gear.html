Gear Guide

Board types, and a tool to help you pick the right wetsuit.

Common Board Types

Board Type Typical Length Best For
Longboard 9–11 ft Beginners; smooth, stable rides on smaller waves
Funboard / Mini-mal 7–8.5 ft Progressing surfers moving toward more maneuverability
Shortboard 5.5–7 ft Experienced surfers; sharp turns and steeper waves
Fish 5–6.5 ft Small, weak waves; extra width for speed and float
Gun 7–10 ft Large, fast, powerful waves
